## Judging and merge gate

1. For each candidate, report A and B counts of keep, revise, reject, deterministic suppression and incomplete verdict, plus proof status. Compare keep-or-revise shares; source-adjudicate every majority change. Follow each expected behavior through verification, grouping and final publication. The single Opus pair per public capture tests for a cross-family regression and cannot estimate per-candidate majority rates.
2. On public cases, independently judge every published fix and test against the source and fixing change. Classify each as consistent with the real fix, a different valid remedy, or wrong. For a control or additional finding without a known fix, verify the remedy against the actual contract. For each model and each advice type (fix, test), report **wrong published suggestions / all published suggestions** for A and B, with raw numerators, denominators and absent-advice counts. Split A by its assessment label. A false finding and wrong advice on a real finding are separate outcomes.

   Test A's gate directly. Read the verifier-final fix/test sources in `composition-sources.json` that the assessment label made ineligible for publication. Exclude historical provenance-only sources, missing text, eligible advice the composer omitted, and text actually published in that group. Deduplicate identical withheld text within a review, finding group and advice type. Adjudicate it with the same rubric, initially blind to whether it was published or withheld. Compare A's wrong share among withheld suggestions with its wrong share among published suggestions, by model and advice type, showing counts and how many independent cases contribute. A clearly higher withheld share is evidence for retaining a gate and testing the flat-label fallback. Similar shares provide no evidence of discrimination; sparse cells or empty denominators leave the result unresolved. [Approved correction](https://github.com/0xPolygon/codegenie/issues/146#issuecomment-7064661256)
3. Per 100 Stage 9 verifier requests, measure padded calls, detector trips, timeouts and field repairs with the same definitions in A and B. Record argument sizes, wall time including grace, incomplete verdicts and publication costs. Recompute A after #143. Inspect whether padding moved to another field. Aggregate Stage 9 time by the mean within each input/arm, then give inputs equal weight within the existing and public strata. Show per-input times and both model totals. Report Stages 5–7 capture costs separately from downstream replay costs.
4. **Kill** on an adjudicated, change-attributed loss of an expected finding that A publishes in most draws on that input; a wholly false B finding never published by A on that input; or any confirmed false B finding of medium severity or higher on a negative control. Stop and retain the observed spend. On the Opus cross-check, source-adjudicate a discordant pair and send any credible loss to the operator before step 2.
5. **Success requires all four conditions:** no kill, lower padded-call **and** field-repair rates, no increase in Stage 9 wall time, and no evidence of poorer advice quality by model and advice type. Report the wrong shares and raw counts for all four cells. If B's observed wrong share is higher in a cell, use A's direct gate test to decide it. A clearly higher wrong share among A's withheld advice than among A's published advice fails the condition and leads to retaining the gate and evaluating the flat per-suggestion enum. If those A shares are alike, the cell is unproven and goes to the operator. A higher B sample share alone does not trigger fallback. A clearly discriminating gate also warrants the fallback when B's observed share happens to be lower. Take contrary Opus evidence, an empty denominator, ambiguous attribution or a sparse tie to the operator before integration.
