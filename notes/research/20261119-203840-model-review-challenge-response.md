# Provider and fast-lane review after challenge

This amends the [provider workstream](./20261119-201523-model-providers-fast-lane.md) in response to the [independent challenge](./20261119-203826-challenge-model-providers-fast-lane.md). The proposed first fast lane remains an experiment with light depth and independent verification. Its saving and its recall on the shipping build have yet to be measured.

**Claude compatibility.** Named-choice rejection is a property of the affected model. The Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2 checks established that their serialized named requests failed and otherwise identical automatic requests succeeded. Separate official documentation extends the restriction to Sonnet 5.5 and Fable/Mythos 5.1. The challenger also captured a named request for Sonnet 5.5. The original 341 passing tests did not cover these catalog entries. Issue #46's ticket-branch fix uses the operator's chosen conservative catalog rule for Anthropic Messages, retaining configured reasoning on the auto path. Fable 5 accepts forced choice but also takes auto under the rule. Haiku 5.5 accepts it and stays forced. Bedrock uses a separate wire protocol. Its independent compatibility issue remains open. [Live controls](./20261119-202649-anthropic-token-count-confirmation.md) · [Vendor release notes](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/release-notes/overview) · [Direct provider issue](https://github.com/0xPolygon/codegenie/issues/46) · [Bedrock issue](https://github.com/0xPolygon/codegenie/issues/47)

**Credential preflight.** The challenger reproduced the bearer-only Pi success and codegenie refusal. Plan 125 deliberately excluded the bearer token from the API-key lookup because it uses a different HTTP header. Preserve that choice. Codegenie also uses the lookup when deciding whether a model is available, which yields the confirmed false negative. The product decision is whether codegenie intends to support that ambient credential through the ordinary CLI. Record the observed mismatch and disputed defect classification separately. Never pass the bearer token as an API key to work around it. [Placement decision](../../specs/plans/125-issue-125-github-model-aliases-and-provider-credentials.md#L195) · [Current lookup](../../src/provider/pi-ai-models.ts#L111)

**Stage accounting.** Compare billed or nominal dollars first and identify the serving path. In the two local examples Stage 7 accounted for 77.8% and 94.8% of observed dollars. Cache reads made up 50.0% and 37.5% of total tokens. Excluding those reads, the Stage 7 token shares were 80.4% and 94.9%. The first run's combined stages 1–4 took 0.62 seconds. The second run stopped at the token budget before dispatching 98 packets. Its shares describe work performed by that point. The third, failed trace recorded 559 Stage 7 attempts including 202 transient errors. The challenger's uncontrolled 0.8.0 sample of 20 runs gives a Stage 7 range of 54–93% of dollars, and a combined planner/composer range of 7–46% on small diffs. The separate historical adaptive-on cohort had median Stage 7 and Stage 9 cost shares of 42.0% and 46.2%. A control with identical model, input and settings is required to estimate savings from a lane. [Archive analysis](./20261119-203450-archive-frequency-and-stage-cost.md)

**Light depth.** The planner sees depth in its dossier. The same hunk can consequently receive a different coverage decision, different lenses or a different skip decision. Coverage escalation also uses depth when recomputing tool allowances. The verifier uses its own fixed tool budget scaled by the budget multiplier. Compare the distribution of planner assignments, routed skills, packet count, escalations, incomplete work and verification volume alongside the usual cost and recall metrics. The existing studies identified no light-depth scored arm. Preserve the existing closeout transcript and require genuine independent verification. Stage 8's historical contribution concerned resolved hints. Any proposed deletion needs adjudication of the 17 resolutions as well as a positive control on which targeted follow-up can add value. [Dossier](../../src/pipeline/planner.ts#L113) · [Escalation](../../src/pipeline/coverage-escalation.ts#L43) · [Verifier budget](../../src/pipeline/verifier.ts#L843)

**Paid experiment, pending owner approval.** Freeze one build after the scorer fixes. Obtain an actual normal-depth, adaptive-off reference on each case with the shipping lens policy, K=1, verification enabled, budget multiplier 1, local call cache disabled, and worker and provider concurrency both 6. Hold all of those steady for a normal/adaptive-on arm and a light/adaptive-off arm. Interleave arms for three repetitions per arm on three immutable cases, including an independently adjudicated non-Go case with a cross-file defect and safe controls. This yields 27 runs on one family, 54 across two. Use the direct CI `anthropic/claude-opus-5:high` path and native `openai-codex/gpt-6-astra:high` path. Both advertise high in the pinned catalog. Codex cost estimates are nominal. Record realized spend and wall time from the pilot before approving further repeats. The proposed maxima of ten repeats for the smaller and hard cases and five for the large case would total 150 runs across three arms and two families.

Compare paired expectation outcomes, severity of adjudicated misses and new false positives, budget health, local/provider cache accounting, median and range of elapsed time and cost, including unknown-cost calls. A three-run pilot supplies cost and feasibility estimates. Default changes and deletions require adjudication of material discordances, enough repetitions to distinguish variance, and cross-family confirmation for a global claim. A small tied sample, or two extra successes selected from many case/expectation comparisons, cannot establish equivalence or warrant a global change. A light arm must lower both cost and elapsed time without recurrent material misses or increased incomplete coverage. Record ambiguity as such.

**Other provider findings.** The challenger found no contrary evidence for the serialized differences on Gemini, Mistral and Radius. Gemini 2.5 Flash uses a numeric thinking budget on ordinary calls and a thinking level on forced calls. Non-reasoning Codestral receives a reasoning-effort field on forced calls. Radius drops the selected effort on forced calls. These are adapter discrepancies confirmed without network access. Service rejection and effects on review quality remain unmeasured for those three paths.
