# Challenge of the deep-review synthesis

I reviewed the [synthesis](./20261119-203637-synthesis-deep-review.md) against master f240376, the original issue proposals, the owner-authorized provider controls and the independently read evaluation artifacts. The revised #31 design includes the strict failure guarantee, the selected #46 fix reflects the operator's decision, and the revised cost comparison recognizes that verification can cost as much as packet review. I support the first fast-lane experiment with these remaining corrections.

1. **Make the paid proposal internally consistent and explicitly subject to owner approval.** Section 4 currently lists three historical cases *plus* a new non-Go case, then calculates 54 pilot runs from three cases. A four-case pilot is 72 runs, with unpriced fourth-case cost. I recommend exactly three for the first round: small, larger, and a hard cross-file case in a second language with adjudicated safe behavior. If the historical hard case remains a fourth, cost and schedule it separately. The second-language case has no observed unit price. Label its cost unknown until the first paid block. The cap is proposed. No spend approval was granted for this matrix. Fix the failed-run and missing-output scoring defects before relying on pass/fail. The [workstream B response](./20261119-203840-model-review-challenge-response.md) specifies the exact provider/model routes, high reasoning, shipped worker and provider concurrency of six, three arms and paired repetition.
2. **State what the observed data population can support.** The 92/92 adaptive-on count applies to two longitudinal series. A separate 12-trace 0.5.0 experiment in July omitted the setting. The conclusion that controlled 0.8.0 recall at product defaults is unmeasured stands. Uncontrolled 0.8.0 telemetry does exist, including 20 runs in the paired challenger's census and a public CI run. Keep operational measurements and controlled recall evidence separately identified. The 10 supplemental-source-only published findings are attributions within an on-arm and cannot establish causal recall loss with the pass off. Counts for route passes, verifier keeps and adjudicated true positives have distinct denominators. [Stage census and population](./20261119-203450-archive-frequency-and-stage-cost.md) · [Independent provider challenge](./20261119-203826-challenge-model-providers-fast-lane.md)
3. **Separate harmless deletion from a behavior change without measured yield.** Stage 8 resolved 17 concerns across nine full active traces. Removing it changes downstream questions even though the archived runs yielded zero new candidates. Count correct and unsupported resolutions as well as uniquely surfaced findings in an active positive control. The Stage 7 candidate cleanup ran only twice in the cited 35-invalid-submit population, with zero recoveries. Two exposures give little power to declare it inert. Scrutinize current-family inputs and explicitly replay matching failure shapes before deleting. Both decisions differ from removing a function with no callers. Quantitative claims about one-third of the pipeline or thousands of lines being safe to delete need a reproducible scope and call-site accounting, or should be marked as estimates. [Pipeline measurement follow-up](./20261119-203132-pipeline-architecture-measurements-and-replies.md) · [Code audit challenge response](./20261119-203000-code-audit-challenge-response.md)
4. **Measure the planner when testing light depth.** Stage 5 includes depth in its dossier and therefore its model prompt. Stage 6 uses depth when escalating tool budgets. Stage 9 has the same budget per verifier, but the number and identity of candidates entering it can change. Record coverage/skip distributions, resulting packet count, escalated packets and candidate counts under each arm. Label the 20% supplemental cost as historical attribution and elapsed wave savings as a hypothesis. A plan-diff table on paired inputs reveals whether fast results were purchased by skipping more eligible hunks. [Planner dossier](../../src/pipeline/planner.ts#L113) · [Escalation](../../src/pipeline/coverage-escalation.ts#L43) · [Verifier](../../src/pipeline/verifier.ts#L843)

Its reuse in ordinary model preflight produces a reproduced false negative. Record the disputed product scope before treating it as a confirmed defect. Three separate forced-submit translations and the effective-reasoning telemetry discrepancy have offline wire evidence. Name wire-level outcomes without suggesting an untested provider HTTP failure. The owner requested separate issues for distinct confirmed symptoms. A row or delivery PR can group related implementation while retaining separate issue identities. [Paired response](./20261119-203840-model-review-challenge-response.md) · [Token-count controls](./20261119-202649-anthropic-token-count-confirmation.md)

A documented managed product can support verifier retention as a design choice without proving industry convergence or the absence of all confidence filters. For a proposed base-revision policy, resolve the repository-configured base-branch bootstrap, the first-commit empty-tree case and the behavior of user-specified extra skill paths before describing it as one mode-independent change or estimating its size. **Decision rule.** Use the three repetitions first to price the comparison and examine discordances. Extend a meaningful ambiguous result to the predeclared maxima of ten repetitions for small/hard cases and five for the large case, within a separately authorized budget. Compare verified material outcomes and adjudicated false positives, not merely overall pass counts. For the fast lane, require lower dollars and lower wall time plus no repeatable material recall/precision regression or additional incomplete work on the tested cases. If the larger corpus remains tied or too sparse, retain the current default and label the decision unresolved. The measured archive cannot authorize an automatic deletion from a 4/5 versus 5/5 result.
