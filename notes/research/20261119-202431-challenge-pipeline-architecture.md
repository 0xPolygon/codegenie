# Challenge of the pipeline architecture audit

The [architecture workstream](20261119-202121-pipeline-architecture.md) identifies distinct Stage 9 and Stage 10 loss mechanisms, finds the unreachable legacy note suppression independently, and makes a reproducible case for retaining a report on a composer failure. I would narrow its highest-ranked recommendation and amend the following claims before synthesis.

## 1. Change publication after fixing the verdict contract

**B1 should be split.** Stage 9 requires established proof and required evidence for live keep/revise, and rejects essential unresolved assumptions. It still accepts `falsePositiveRisk: "high"` with those fields, and its proof is a structured model judgment. Making keep/revise sufficient for publication under that current contract would widen the false-positive path. First enforce the existing risk contract and distinguish genuine verification from disabled verification. The disabled branch synthesizes `keep`, required evidence and low risk. I passed a low-confidence, evidence-backed changed-line candidate through the real Stage 9 and Stage 10 functions with `verify: false` and offline deterministic fallback. The result was `verificationSkipped: true` with the candidate published as inline at low confidence. Run health was correctly incomplete. The local policy still used its verified exception. [Verifier producer, lines 255–295](../../src/pipeline/verifier.ts#L255) · [Keep contract, lines 793–818 and 838–854](../../src/pipeline/verifier.ts#L793) · [Publication exception, lines 1696–1738](../../src/pipeline/composer.ts#L1696) · [Spec keep criteria, line 1479](../../specs/project/architecture.md#L1479)

Removing the post-verification confidence cutoff changes which already verified candidates publish. Removing the pre-verification lane changes who reaches Stage 9 and the amount of work it can dispatch. These need different evidence. Replay annotated keeps for the first and compare dispatch, spend, candidate-level adjudication and required recall for the second. Report novel findings to a reviewer independently of whether the scorers know them. Pooled precision of published candidates is a weak reference here because category mix and an earlier filter already selected the cohorts. Stratify at least by promoted versus direct, risk and severity. **Tests.** One table across verify on/off, established/essential proof, high versus low risk, merged representative versus sibling, and changed-line versus anchorless publication. Require inline placement to honor `minInlineConfidence` in every branch. Add a medium/low candidate that never reaches verification because of the current pre-gate, and treat the existing standard/evidence-resolution capacity as part of the outcome. Keep a phase's change only when both recall and false-positive adjudication justify it. ## 2. Keep an evidence requirement before Stage 8 removes a question

**The legacy deletion is safe and another live suppression mechanism remains.** Stage 8 accepts `resolvedHints` on nonblank prose, uses the task question over the returned question, fills missing scope from the task, and removes matching Stage 7 hints before promotion and evidence-backed Stage 10 reconciliation. A submitted resolution such as “I could not establish what the caller does” can remove the concern, and there is no evidence field on that submission shape. Scope matching also truncates files and symbols at five. Deleting the dead Stage 10 path is a pure deletion. Changing Stage 8's resolution requires an explicit decision. Reuse the evidence-backed concern handoff or keep a question when stage 8 does not supply support for resolution. **Tests.** Two packets with the same question, a fabricated conclusion, a candid unresolved result, a genuinely established answer, and a second concern with the same initial scope elements. Assert which concerns reach promotion and the final report. ## 3. Coverage guarantee should mean attribution and disclosure

**The reported path and reason disappear for a planner skip.** My offline probe sent one source hunk and a reasoned `skip` through `aggregateRunCoverage` and the public coverage renderer. It returned `partial: false`, `skipped: 1`, `excluded: 1`, no per-path gaps and no reasons. It displayed “Reviewed 0/1 hunks,” a generic exclusion count, and the depth counts. The artifact ledger can list the path and reason. That qualifies the architecture note's promise that the model cannot hide an unreviewed hunk, and supports #31. Keep filtering and planner skip separately attributable, with per-path reasons. Test a mixed run with one deterministic exclusion, one model skip and one incomplete worker. Test a transient provider error repaired within one call, an exhausted provider retry, a missing reservation, and a non-retryable proof rejection. Require genuine extra recovery before enabling full reruns.
